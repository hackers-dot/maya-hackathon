MAYA

Before MAYA starts

MAYA will (read-only checks)

  • Read the HTTP response headers your website sends.
  • Read the flags on the cookies your website sets.
  • Check whether files such as .env, .git and backups are publicly reachable, using GET requests only.
  • Read your public DNS records for email settings (SPF and DMARC).

MAYA will NOT

  • Run exploits or send attack payloads.
  • Brute force or guess passwords.
  • Change anything on your website without your approval.

Checks run only on a website you have verified you own.
