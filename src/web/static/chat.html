      How can MAYA help today?

      Security checks and answers for the sites you own.

      Scope guard on Read-only checks Fixes need your approval

        Ask MAYA anything about web security. It answers about your own sites only from verified findings.

        Upload

        Your official domains and phone numbers

        Verified websites count automatically. One entry per line. Saved lists are shown masked.

        Source code from GitHub

        Paste the address of a PUBLIC repository: github.com/owner/repo, or github.com/owner/repo/tree/branch for one branch. MAYA downloads the repository ZIP from codeload.github.com (no token, read-only, at most 25 MB), reads it in memory and never runs it. The repository name is sent to GitHub. One fetch per 10 seconds.

        Watch for lookalike domains

        Enter one of your own domains (a verified website or a domain on your official list). MAYA builds up to 40 lookalike names (typos, look-alike characters, hyphens, other endings, your name plus words like "support" or "login") and checks the top 25.

        Passive only: MAYA never visits these domains. The candidate names are sent to cloudflare-dns.com (DNS records) and crt.sh (certificate records), at most one request per second.

        Your data

        • What is stored: your account (email and a scrypt password hash), what you connected (website addresses, upload file names), scan results with their evidence records (secrets masked before storing), your chats, and an audit log of approvals.
        • Uploads: ZIP and log files are read in memory and never saved. Only findings with masked excerpts are kept.
        • Screenshots (suspicious message): sent to the model provider only after you confirm. MAYA keeps the image's hash, size and the masked text read from it; the image itself only if you tick "include image", and then only in memory until the server restarts or you delete your data. Your official domains and phone numbers are stored with your account.
        • Where: only on this computer, in MAYA's local data folder (SQLite and JSON files). Exceptions you control: with a model key, findings and chat messages go to the model provider; the SPF/DMARC check sends your domain name to cloudflare-dns.com.
        • How long: until you delete it here. Scan runs are held in memory and end when the server restarts; evidence records and chats stay until deleted.

        Delete my data

        Deletes your connected assets (and their verified status), scan runs, evidence records, chats and your demo app's fix state. Your account stays so you can sign in again; one audit entry with counts only (no content) records the deletion.

        DEMO: these results come from MAYA's deliberately vulnerable demo app on loopback, not a real site.
